Where the Moats Are: AI Competitive Advantage, Late 2026

SmallPE Investment Team · October 26, 2026 · 4 pages

The AI capability race split into two separate races. Open-weight models won reach, carrying the large majority of commodity inference volume. Closed frontier labs won revenue, tripling enterprise spend capture by monetizing a narrow, persistent capability lead. We argue the asset worth underwriting is no longer the model at all — it has moved to proprietary data, evaluation infrastructure, and graded agentic trajectories — and we set out exactly how that changes our underwriting, diligence, and sourcing.

Key findings

  • The open/closed capability gap has held at roughly four months for a year running — a stable, non-closing lead, not a trend toward zero.
  • Open-weight models carry the large majority of commodity inference volume; closed frontier labs capture the large majority of enterprise production spend.
  • The durable moat has relocated to proprietary domain data, task-specific evaluation infrastructure, and graded agentic trajectories — not model weights.
  • We now run six diligence tests on every AI-exposed target, and discount terminal value on thin, single-model-wrapper revenue.
  • A parallel dislocation may be forming at the compute/infrastructure layer heading into 2027 — covered as an open, source-flagged question inside the paper.
