AI Strategy Research Note October 26, 2026

Where the Moats Are: AI Competitive Advantage, Late 2026

The AI capability race split into two: open-weight models won reach, while closed frontier labs won revenue by monetizing a stable, ~4-month capability lead. We argue the durable moat has moved off the model entirely — into proprietary data, evaluation infrastructure, and graded agentic trajectories — and translate that into a concrete underwriting, diligence, and sourcing framework for lower-middle-market investing.
