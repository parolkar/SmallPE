# Fund 1 Value Creation Playbook

## Executive Summary

This playbook defines SmallPE's AI-first approach to driving measurable value across the portfolio post-acquisition. Rather than relying on traditional human-heavy operating teams, SmallPE deploys AI agents alongside operators to compress value creation timelines from years to months—targeting a 30-40% OPEX reduction within 6 months, 2-3x revenue growth within 18 months, and multiple expansion from 2.5x to 6-8x ARR.

The playbook is built around four pillars: (1) AI-Powered Operational Excellence, (2) Product Enhancement, (3) Go-To-Market Acceleration, and (4) Impact & Governance—each owned by a dedicated SmallPE AI agent and executed against standardized milestones.

---

## Pillar 1: AI-Powered Operational Excellence (Months 0-6)

**Owner:** Operating Partner Agent
**Goal:** 30-40% OPEX reduction while maintaining or improving customer experience.

### 1.1 Customer Support Automation
- Deploy AI support agent trained on historical tickets, docs, and codebase.
- Target: 70% ticket deflection via auto-resolution.
- Escalation path to human CSM for high-value accounts.

### 1.2 Sales & Lead Intelligence
- Implement AI lead scoring and routing using product usage data.
- Automate CRM hygiene and enrichment (firmographic, technographic).
- Generate weekly pipeline health reports.

### 1.3 Finance & Back-Office
- Standardize financial reporting (P&L, MRR, churn cohorts).
- Automate accounts payable/receivable and revenue recognition.
- Centralize via shared services across portfolio.

### 1.4 Engineering Productivity
- Introduce AI pair-programming and code review agents.
- Standardize CI/CD, monitoring, and security scanning.
- Target: 2x developer throughput.

**Exit Criteria for Phase 1:**
- [ ] EBITDA margin improved by 15+ points
- [ ] AI support agent live with <12h MTTR
- [ ] Standardized dashboard for board reporting

---

## Pillar 2: Product Enhancement (Months 6-12)

**Owner:** Product Strategy Agent
**Goal:** Unlock 25-35% pricing power through AI-native feature additions.

### 2.1 AI Feature Layer
- Wrap existing Rails APIs with AI capabilities (copilots, summaries, predictions).
- Ship first AI feature within 90 days of close.

### 2.2 Usage-Based Analytics
- Instrument product with AI-driven insight layer.
- Surface adoption, retention, and expansion signals per account.

### 2.3 Intelligent Automation Workflows
- Build customer-facing automation templates.
- Reduce time-to-value for new onboards by 50%.

### 2.4 Security & Compliance Hardening
- SOC 2 Type II readiness.
- Automated data governance via AI classification.

**Exit Criteria for Phase 2:**
- [ ] At least 2 AI-native features GA
- [ ] Net Revenue Retention (NRR) >115%
- [ ] Price uplift applied to >50% of customer base

---

## Pillar 3: Go-To-Market Acceleration (Months 12-18)

**Owner:** Growth Partner Agent
**Goal:** 2-3x revenue growth via scalable, AI-assisted GTM motions.

### 3.1 AI Content & Demand Gen
- Programmatic SEO and content at scale via AI agents.
- Personalized outbound with AI-generated sequences.

### 3.2 Self-Serve & PLG
- Launch self-serve trial flow.
- AI onboarding assistant to drive activation.

### 3.3 Partner & Channel
- AI-matched partner introductions (integrations, resellers).
- Co-marketing motion with portfolio peers.

### 3.4 Expansion Plays
- Land-and-expand framework with AI-identified upsell triggers.
- Multi-product bundling across portfolio where relevant.

**Exit Criteria for Phase 3:**
- [ ] ARR growth of 2x vs. entry
- [ ] CAC payback < 12 months
- [ ] 3+ repeatable acquisition channels

---

## Pillar 4: Impact & Governance (Ongoing)

**Owner:** Iris (Impact Co-GP Agent)
**Goal:** Institutionalize impact investing with measurable outcomes.

### 4.1 Impact Framework
- Assign IRIS+ aligned metrics per portfolio company.
- Quarterly impact scoring via Iris agent.

### 4.2 Responsible AI
- Bias and safety audits on every shipped AI feature.
- Customer data protection standards enforced portfolio-wide.

### 4.3 Founder & Team Enablement
- Founder retention/advisory program (12-month structured handover).
- Shared services talent pool (engineering, GTM, finance).
- Annual portfolio summit and peer learning forums.

| Cadence       | Activity                                      | Lead                        |
|---------------|-----------------------------------------------|-----------------------------|
| Weekly        | Operator sync + AI agent dashboard review     | Operating Partner Agent     |

---

## Standardized KPIs (Per Portfolio Company)

**Financial:** ARR, EBITDA margin, NRR, CAC payback, gross margin.
**Product:** Feature adoption, time-to-value, AI feature attach rate.
**Operational:** Ticket deflection %, developer throughput, churn cohort.
**Impact:** IRIS+ scorecard, responsible AI audit pass rate.

---

## 100-Day Plan (Per Acquisition)

1. **Days 1-30:** Onboard operators, deploy AI support agent, baseline KPIs.
2. **Days 31-60:** Standardize financials and reporting, ship first AI feature.
3. **Days 61-100:** Launch lead scoring, finalize product roadmap, set impact targets.

## Summary

SmallPE's value creation model treats AI not as a tool but as a teammate. By pairing specialized agents with seasoned operators and applying a standardized, phased playbook, the firm systematically drives margin expansion, product enhancement, and revenue growth—while embedding impact accountability from day one. The result: faster, more repeatable, and more transparent value creation across the portfolio.
