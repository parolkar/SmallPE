The team: one Managing Partner and eight specialists

  1. Oracle

    Investment thesis & macro trends

    Synthesises macroeconomic, technology and social trends into defensible theses, so the fund invests ahead of the curve.

  2. Helios

    Deal sourcing & screening

    Turns Oracle's theses into a qualified, proprietary pipeline in the lower-middle market.

  3. Athena

    Due diligence orchestrator

    Runs diligence as a structured, data-driven exercise, with Prometheus and Themis reporting in.

  4. Prometheus

    Business evaluation

    Writes growth-oriented evaluations that surface value-creation opportunities and name the challenges honestly.

  5. Themis

    Evidence verification

    Checks every claim in the evaluation against documented, verifiable evidence. No hopeful assumptions.

  6. Logos

    Financial modelling & valuation

    Moves models from static spreadsheets to probabilistic scenarios, so the range of outcomes is visible before the bid.

  7. Hermes

    Financing & deal closing

    Compares lender proposals, automates the routine closing work and keeps the deal on schedule.

  8. Iris

    Impact investing

    Engages at every stage and measures impact with IRIS+, so the firm never claims what it cannot measure.

[ Why SmallPE ]

SmallPE exists to prove that a small, AI-native team can outperform traditional private equity in the lower-middle market.

Deal velocity over bureaucracy

Automate the routine. Spend human judgment where it matters.

Evidence over opinion

Every decision rests on verified, documented evidence, and every IC report defends itself.

Impact integrity

Impact as a competitive advantage, not marketing. Nothing claimed that cannot be measured.

[ Deploy your own ]

Your own AI-first firm,
live in about five minutes.

For a team · hosted

One click on Railway

  1. Click Deploy. The form asks for an admin password and your OpenRouter key.
  2. Wait for the first build, about five minutes. Volume, domain and health check come with the template.
  3. Sign in as admin and talk to the Managing Partner.
Deploy on Railway
On your machine · Node.js 22.19+

Run it locally

$ git clone https://github.com/parolkar/SmallPE
$ cd SmallPE
$ ./bin/install.sh   # the harness + all 9 agents
$ ./bin/run.sh       # open the dsh web: URL it prints

Everything installs inside the folder. Delete it and it is all gone.

Prefer something else? Start from the Replit template, try the SmallPE Grok Bot, or read the full deployment guide.
