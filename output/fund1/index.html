SmallPE Fund 0.1

Let's Help AI
Build a PE Fund.

A few members of the VCPE community came together and asked a fun but bold question.

"If AI can run a retail shop autonomously — why not a PE fund?"

Nobody has a good answer. So we decided to find it out together — not by talking about it, but by taking action. Start with what we know, and shape it together. Purely for fun and learning, applying the concepts we pick up from each other. Singapore is small. The Private Equity community is small. Why experiment with AI in a silo? Let's learn together.

Express Interest How It Works

What Is This?

Private equity is one of the most opaque industries in finance. Decisions happen behind closed doors. Frameworks stay proprietary. The learning curve for new entrants is steeper than it needs to be.

We're running an educational experiment to change that. A group of practitioners from the VCPE community are coming together to design a fund from scratch — operated by a Human+AI team — and document every decision openly as we go.

We start as a fictional agentic fund operating team: designing the thesis, the deal process, the IC structure, and the playbooks — with AI agents doing the heavy lifting alongside us. If everything falls into place, the goal is for this to grow into a real fund. But either way, the learning is real.

What gets documented openly

  • Investment thesis formation and revisions
  • Deal sourcing criteria and how AI runs the pipeline
  • Due diligence frameworks and live evaluation outputs
  • IC meeting formats and decision rationale
  • Operational playbooks and AI agent configurations
  • What didn't work — and why

How It Works

20 Practitioners

A deliberately small cohort. Quality over scale. Every member brings real VCPE experience and actively shapes the fund's decisions — not a passive audience.

AI-First Operations

The SmallPE agent team — Oracle, Helios, Athena, Logos, Hermes, Iris — handles deal sourcing, diligence, modeling, and IC reporting. Practitioners steer the agents, not spreadsheets.

Radical Openness

Everything the cohort produces — frameworks, playbooks, IC reports, agent configs — is published openly. The fund's operating manual becomes a public resource for the field.

Who Should Join

Everyone is welcome to follow along. If you'd like to be part of the active team building this, here's who we're hoping to hear from.

VC / PE Professionals

Associates, VPs, Principals, or Partners who have worked on real deals and want to rethink how the work gets done with AI.

Operators & Founders

People who've built and scaled companies, and want to understand capital allocation from the GP side.

AI Practitioners in Finance

Engineers or data scientists applying AI to financial workflows — your technical perspective strengthens the cohort's output.

Fund Lawyers & Advisors

Those with fund formation, regulatory, or LP relations experience who can stress-test the operational and legal design.
